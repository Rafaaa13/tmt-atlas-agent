## Quality notes

- `verified_facts.json` contains 17 facts for three companies: Baidu, Alibaba and Meta. It does not fabricate coverage for the remaining demo companies.
- Each fact preserves period start/end, fiscal-year convention, currency/unit, accounting basis, consolidation/attribution scope, source URL, release title/date and a section/table locator.
- Numeric facts are paraphrased rather than copied as long quotations. No quotation field is used.
- Alibaba and Baidu accounting labels are document-specific. Baidu’s release uses GAAP/non-GAAP language but does not, in the inspected article, warrant an automatic “IFRS” label; Alibaba’s release supplies U.S. GAAP reconciliation context. Meta explicitly identifies U.S. GAAP.
- `demo_seed.json` remains illustrative demo data and was intentionally left unchanged; `evt006` was not used.
